=>>> Additional Non-Generic Enhancements for WaveTone =>>>

4> What More Can Be Added (Still Non-Generic)
> These features focus on system design innovation rather than generic chat functionality.
> They enhance conversation quality, moderation fairness, and academic depth.


1> Conversation Phase Control (Very Unique)

> Rooms are divided into defined phases:
> Intro
> Open Discussion
> Summary

> Only specific actions are allowed during each phase.
> This enforces structured and focused conversations.

Notes
> No AI is required.
> Highly innovative from a system-design perspective.


2> AI-Based Conversation Summary (Post-Room)

> After the room expires, AI generates a short textual summary.
> No audio is permanently stored.
> Only abstracted discussion points are retained.

Pros
> Extremely impressive feature
> Highly useful for study and discussion rooms

Cons
> Requires careful privacy framing and explanation


3> AI-Based Speaker Balance Insight

> After the room ends, the system provides insights such as:
> “Conversation was dominated by 2 speakers”
> “Participation was evenly distributed”

> Analysis is based only on audio metadata.
> No speech content is analyzed or stored.


4> Smart Vote-Kick Assistance (AI-lite)

> AI highlights moderation-relevant patterns such as:
> “This user has received the most complaints”
> “This user frequently violates topic focus”

> Final moderation decisions are always made by users, not AI.


=>>> Academic Framing of AI Usage =>>>

Never say
> “AI controls the chat”
> “AI monitors users”

Always say
> “AI assists moderation decisions”
> “AI augments user-driven governance”
> “AI operates on metadata or temporary buffers”
